My neighbor had been stealing packages from my porch for months. Every time I ordered something online, it would mysteriously disappear before I got home from work.

I reported it to the police, but they said without proof, there was nothing they could do. So I decided to take matters into my own hands.

I ordered a glitter bomb kit online and set up a hidden security camera. When the package arrived, I made sure to leave it on the porch for exactly thirty minutes.

Like clockwork, my neighbor came over and snatched it. But this time, I was ready.

The glitter bomb was pressure-activated. The moment they opened that box in their living room, fine iridescent glitter exploded everywhere. It covered their furniture, their carpet, even got into their air vents.

The best part? My camera captured everything. Their face when they realized what had happened was priceless.

I printed the photos and left them in their mailbox with a note that said "I know what you've been doing. This stops now."

They never stole another package again. In fact, they couldn't even look me in the eye anymore.

Sometimes the best revenge is the kind that keeps on giving. Every time they vacuum, they probably find more glitter.
